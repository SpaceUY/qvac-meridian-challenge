What is the enterprise P1 first-response SLA?

4 hours for production down / safety interlock on ControLink Suite and ServoDrive X4 enterprise contracts.

What about P2 and P3?

  • P2 (major feature impaired): 8 hours first response; workaround/restore target 72 hours.
  • P3 (minor defect / question): 1 business day first response; restore best effort.

How did we do in Q2?

P1 mean first response was 2.6 hours (within SLA). Two breaches occurred on 3 May and 19 May during an APAC on-call gap. That roster gap closed 15 June 2026.

Where is the escalation matrix?

See policies/escalation-matrix.txt. For P1: L1→L2 within 30 minutes; L2→Engineering within 2 hours.

Can I promise a ControLink Gateway rev C next week?

No — CAPA-441 ship-hold: do not promise rev C delivery before 15 August 2026 without written Ops approval. Prefer rev B for July needs.
