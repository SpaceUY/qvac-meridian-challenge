### 1. Executive summary

Meridian Components delivered a strong second quarter. **Total recognized revenue was $18.4M**, against a board-approved target of **$17.0M**, an **8.2%** beat (**$1.4M** above plan). New ARR bookings for the quarter were **$3.9M**, including the **Atlas Manufacturing** enterprise agreement at **$2.35M ARR** (closed **12 June 2026**).

Gross margin finished at **34.5%**. Logo churn was **3.1%** (within the ≤ 3.5% guardrail). Company NPS from the June pulse survey was **47** (n = 186). The locked **Q3 2026 revenue forecast is $19.8M**, with a **new ARR bookings target of $4.2M**.
