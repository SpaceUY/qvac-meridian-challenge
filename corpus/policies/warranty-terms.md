# Meridian Components Inc. — Standard Warranty Terms (Summary)

**Document ID:** LEG-WAR-2026-004  
**Effective:** 1 January 2026  
**Owner:** Legal · Ops  
**Audience:** Sales, CS, Support

---

## Coverage

1. **Standard warranty** on ServoDrive X4, ControLink Gateway, and ControLink Suite hardware appliances: **24 months** from ship date.
2. **Extended warranty** available at **8% of first-year ARR** (or 8% of hardware list for pure hardware deals), extending coverage to **36 months** total.
3. Software (ControLink Suite seats): warranty covers defect remediation during the paid subscription term; no separate hardware-style ship-date clock.
4. Consumables and wear parts are excluded unless listed on the order as warrantied SKUs.

## Process

- Warranty claims open as Support tickets; P1 production-down still follows the **4-hour** first-response SLA.
- RMA turnaround target: **10 business days** after receipt at the regional depot (Americas: Chicago; EMEA: Munich; APAC: Singapore).

## Notes for field conversations

- Do not invent site-specific warranty lengths. If a customer asks for something outside 24/36 months, route to Legal.
- ControLink Gateway rev C units shipped under CAPA-441 burn-in still carry the standard **24-month** warranty unless the order explicitly purchased extended coverage.
